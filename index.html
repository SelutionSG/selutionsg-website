FAQ

Before you
send the brief.

The questions clients ask most, answered straight.

How far in advance should we book?

Send the brief as early as you can — we quote the same working day, and rosters are confirmed by name about three days before your event. Shorter runway? Tell us anyway; we'll tell you honestly what we can cover with the crew available.

What roles can you supply?

Six tiers, bookable in any mix on one call sheet: ushers & registration, crowd & traffic marshals, setup & logistics crew, promoters & brand ambassadors, AV & technical support, and supervisors & team leads.

How do you make sure crew actually turn up?

Every crew member checks in on arrival through our attendance system — GPS location, timestamp and selfie verification — so gaps are visible before doors open, not after. Our current on-time check-in rate is 92%.

Do crew arrive briefed, or do we have to train them?

Each rostered crew member receives a briefing pack, uniform requirements and call sheet before event day. Your on-ground lead briefs once at call time — not fifty times during the event.

What happens if someone doesn't show?

The assigned supervisor tracks check-ins against the roster from call time and works the gap immediately — that's their job on the floor. You deal with one point of contact, not the no-show.

What do you need from us for a quote?

Event date, venue, roles, headcount, and shift timings — the request form below covers all of it. Rough numbers are fine to start; we'll size the roster with you.

How are hours confirmed for billing?

Check-in and check-out times are logged digitally for every crew member, and a sign-off sheet confirms headcount and hours at wrap. You get the wrap report within 24 hours, so reconciliation is based on records — not memory.
